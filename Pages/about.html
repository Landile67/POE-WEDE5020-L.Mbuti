About Green Haven

Haven Animal Rescue is a registered non-profit organization dedicated to changing lives, one rescue at a time.

Our History

In order to address the escalating problem of animal abuse and homelessness in the Western Cape, Green Haven Dog Rescue was founded in 2018 as a recognised non-profit organization (NPO) in Cape Town, South Africa.

The rescue initially depended solely on community outreach and social media platforms before developing a dedicated digital ecosystem. Over time, the organization has broadened its scope, saving over 1,200 animals and facilitating thousands of spay and neuter procedures to prevent animal overpopulation in underprivileged communities.

🎯 Mission Statement

The goal of Green Haven is to actively promote community education on ethical pet ownership and animal welfare while rescuing, rehabilitating, and finding homes for vulnerable, neglected, and abandoned animals. The organization makes sure every animal has a second opportunity in a secure, caring home by providing thorough veterinary treatment, emergency shelter, and strict adoption matching.

🌟 Vision Statement

Green Haven Animal Rescue wants to end animal homelessness, maltreatment, and needless death in the Western Cape. Green Haven aspires to create a caring society where all pets are respected and safeguarded by growing community spay/neuter clinics, educational seminars, and sustainable donor networks.
