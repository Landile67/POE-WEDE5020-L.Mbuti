Green Haven Dog Rescue is a registered Non-Profit Organisation (NPO 2018/123456/08) operating across Cape Town and the Western Cape. Dedicated to rescuing, rehabilitating, and rehoming vulnerable, abandoned, and neglected animals since 2018.

Official NPO documentation and audited financial disclosures are published in accordance with South African welfare standards. Section 18A tax-deductible donation receipts are available upon request via donations@greenhavendogrescue.org.
